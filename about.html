About Us

Welcome to North Bharat Jobs, launched on 10 Sep 2026.

North Bharat Jobs is an independent job alert portal that provides DIRECT official links for Central Government Jobs (UPSC, SSC, Railway, Banking, Defence) and 8 State Government Jobs: Bihar, Uttar Pradesh, Jharkhand, West Bengal, Delhi, Rajasthan, Uttarakhand, and Himachal Pradesh.

We collect information only from official government websites such as upsc.gov.in, ssc.gov.in, bpsc.bih.nic.in, bssc.bihar.gov.in, uppsc.up.nic.in, upsssc.gov.in, jssc.nic.in, wbpsc.gov.in, dsssb.delhi.gov.in, rpsc.rajasthan.gov.in, psc.uk.gov.in, hppsc.hp.gov.in etc.

Our mission is to provide accurate, fast, and direct official notifications without fake news or misleading information.

We are NOT a government organization. We only aggregate and link to official notifications.
